## Problem

The zero-configuration classifier copied significant title tokens into tags. That produced near-unique tags per book instead of reusable bookstore categories.

## Repair

1. Added a fixed 25-value Korean bookstore taxonomy spanning computer/IT, business, design, humanities, science, and literature.
2. Limited every automatic result to one broad category plus up to two reusable subcategories.
3. Removed title-token, author-name, and proper-noun tag generation.
4. Restricted OpenAI-compatible model output to exact taxonomy values and rejects responses outside the taxonomy.
5. Retained the one-request, one-Spreadsheets-batch folder workflow.
