scenario is `node test/20261124/clip-rail/viewer-clip-browser.mjs`. It locates the
tool and list by role/name and uses engine-specific anchors for page geometry,
the drag preview, and the saved card. Fixture URLs are
`/viewer/clip-test/legacy.pdf?engine=legacy` and
`/viewer/clip-test/gpu.pdf?engine=gpu`. The output directory contains
`result.json`, per-engine traces, and the armed, drag, and completed screenshots.
